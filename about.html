About us

Two people who can't stop making things

Roxy Arts Studio is a small home studio. What started as a hobby turned into a habit: rolling satin ribbon into roses, then gathering them into bouquets and table centrepieces.

Ribbon flowers never wilt, so a bouquet from a wedding, birthday or anniversary can stay on the shelf for years. Every piece is made by hand, so each one is a little different.
